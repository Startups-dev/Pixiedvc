## Key Clauses

- **Point Stewardship** – Owners confirm they control the points required for the stay.
- **Guest Assurance** – HannaDVC screens guest payments and communicates confirmed reservation numbers.
- **Timeline** – Owners supply confirmations within 24 hours; HannaDVC releases payouts after checkout.
- **Cancellations** – Deposits are refunded to guests if no match occurs by the SLA; HannaDVC covers change fees when we adjust itineraries.
